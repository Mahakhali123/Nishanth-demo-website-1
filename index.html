FAQs

Common questions

Can't find the answer? Email us and we'll respond within 24 hours.

What is Nishanth.ai?

Nishanth.ai is an operational AI infrastructure platform that lets you build, train, and deploy AI agents to handle business workflows automatically. Think of it as a team of digital workers that learn exactly how your business operates — and execute tasks faster, more accurately, and around the clock.

How quickly can I deploy my first agent?

Most teams have their first agent live in under 5 minutes. Connect your tools, walk through the guided workflow builder, and deploy. No engineering team required — if you can describe a process in plain English, you can automate it with Nishanth.ai.

What integrations do you support?

We support 400+ integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Slack, Notion, Jira, GitHub, Linear, Stripe, QuickBooks, and many more. Pro and Enterprise plans also support custom API integrations and webhooks.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. Nishanth.ai is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We never use your data to train shared models. Enterprise plans support VPC isolation and on-premise deployments.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes, completely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account dashboard. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period. We don't do lock-ins.

Do you offer a free trial for Pro?

Yes — Pro comes with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. You get full access to all Pro features during the trial. After 14 days, you can continue on the free Starter plan or upgrade to Pro.
